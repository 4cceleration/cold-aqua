About the studio.

A small relaxation studio built around one idea: rest cannot be rushed.

Sereno opened with three treatment rooms, warm linens and no clocks on the walls. Everything since has been chosen to protect the same thing: an hour where nothing at all is asked of you.

We work slowly, one guest at a time, with skilled hands and long appointments. There is no upselling, no rushed turnaround between sessions, and no music you have to endure.

How we work

Every session starts with a short conversation — where the week has landed in your body, how much pressure feels right today, what you would rather we left alone. From there the hour is shaped around you, not around a fixed protocol.

What we promise

  • Licensed therapists, gentle and attentive, in every room.
  • Sound-isolated, low-lit rooms and organic, barely scented oils.
  • Time to linger afterwards, with herbal tea and no hurry.

Booking and cancellations

Appointments run Monday through Saturday and can be booked online. We ask for 24 hours notice for any change so we can offer the hour to another guest. If you are unsure which treatment suits you, our menu of rituals is a good place to start, or you can simply book a short call.

Gift cards

Gift cards are available for any single session or monthly plan, valid for twelve months and transferable. They arrive as a simple card, with no expiry pressure attached.
